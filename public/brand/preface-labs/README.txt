PREFACE LABS — BRAND KIT

Colors
  Void    #0C0D10   primary / backgrounds
  Accent  #FF4D6D   accent (cursor, underscore)
  White   #FFFFFF
  Steel   #5C6170   secondary text
Typeface: JetBrains Mono ExtraBold (800), tracking -4%. Wordmark SVGs are outlined, no font needed.

Where to use what
  Website favicon ....... favicon/ (favicon.ico, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, android-chrome-*)
  Stripe branding ....... png/stripe-icon-512.png (icon), png/stripe-logo-white-bg.png (logo), accent #FF4D6D
  Social avatars ........ png/social-avatar-800.png (platforms crop to circle/rounded)
  Link previews (OG) .... png/og-image-1200x630.png
  Headers / decks ....... svg/pl-lockup-on-light.svg or -on-dark.svg
  Print / vendor ........ svg/ files (vector, scale to any size)

Don't: recolor the P, stretch the mark, or use the accent for body text — keep it for the cursor and underscore.
